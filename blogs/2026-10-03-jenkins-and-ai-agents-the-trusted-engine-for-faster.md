The 2026 Reality Check By 2026, software development has crossed a threshold. AI coding assistants are no longer just suggesting lines of code: agentic systems can take on larger tasks, refactor existing code, open pull requests, and attempt to fix defects with less hands-on direction. That progress revived a familiar prediction: if AI can write and change software, perhaps traditional CI/CD systems will become unnecessary.
